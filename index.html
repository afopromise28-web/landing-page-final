Frequently Asked Questions

What is Unconventional Wisdom?

It is a 30-day devotional for leaders and builders on godly wisdom, built on Proverbs 9:10: “The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom.” It explores how wisdom shapes how we think, decide, speak, work, build, and live before God.

Who is the devotional for?

It is written for young leaders and professionals, builders and visionaries, believers who want practical spiritual growth, readers who want to go deeper in applying Scripture, and teachers and disciplers.

What does being an early reader involve?

You’ll share what you hope to learn, take part in community conversations, and, where asked, give honest feedback. You may also receive updates and, if provided, previews or selected material.

Does joining the early-reader community mean I must buy the book?

No. Joining the community and buying the hardcopy are separate decisions. You can join without ever buying a copy.

Will I have to purchase a hardcopy?

No. The form simply asks whether you’re interested, so we can understand what readers want and plan well.

Is the hardcopy available to order now?

Yes. It’s available on preorder. You can register your interest and we’ll let you know when ordering details are available.

Can I join if I already use another devotional?

Yes. Many readers use more than one. Unconventional Wisdom isn’t meant to replace what already helps you.

Can teachers or disciplers use it?

It is intended to serve as a personal devotional and also as a resource for teaching and discipling others in godly wisdom. The form includes an option to tell us if that’s how you’d use it.
